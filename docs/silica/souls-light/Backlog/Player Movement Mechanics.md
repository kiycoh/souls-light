# Player Movement Mechanics

The player controls movement mechanics that define exploration and combat positioning:

- **Exploration**: The player can move their character on the map to explore environments and evade enemy attacks.
- **Class-Specific Movement**: Movement capabilities may vary by chosen class (e.g., Warrior focuses on melee engagement, Mage on ranged positioning).
- **Directional Control**: For melee attacks, the player (Nox) attacks in the direction of stick tilt, enabling dynamic combat positioning.

Movement is a core mechanic enabling navigation, positioning, and tactical decision-making during runs.

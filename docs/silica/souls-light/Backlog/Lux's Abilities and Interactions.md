# Lux's Abilities and Interactions

Lux, as a playable character, possesses the following core abilities and interactions:

- **Rianimazione (Lux)**: Lux can spend part of their Will energy to revive Nox when HP reaches zero, enabling continued progression in the dungeon.
- **Interazione con Mercante**: Lux can interact with the Merchant NPC to view and purchase permanent power-ups (e.g., Max HP increases) using accumulated currency. Interaction opens a dedicated UI (Scene2D Window) displaying the permanent currency balance.
- **Attivazione Skill**: Lux can activate special abilities via hotkey to gain tactical advantages during combat.

These abilities are designed to enhance survivability and strategic flexibility during runs.

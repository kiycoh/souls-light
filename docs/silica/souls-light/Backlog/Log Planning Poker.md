# Log Planning Poker

Planning Poker is used to estimate the effort required for user stories. In this session:

- The discussion for each user story was contained within 5 minutes.
- The team showed natural engagement and careful evaluation of functional requirements, both conceptually and technically.
- The team normalized the votes: before voting, they reviewed all requirements to form an overall understanding of project difficulty.

This approach ensured a balanced and informed estimation process despite the time constraint.

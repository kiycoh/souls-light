# Ambientazione

Soul’s Light is set in the **Mu**, a vast, empty dimension inhabited by lost souls trapped in eternal limbo. The Mu is a plane of pure potential: unfulfilled memories, unwritten works, nonexistent places, and staircases leading nowhere. Rooms and structures generate procedurally in front of the player.

- **Lore**:
  - The Mu is a state of perpetual waiting. Souls wander without purpose; death in-game respawns the player at the dungeon start for a new run, but permanent death does not exist here.
  - Upon death, souls are condemned to eternal existence in the Mu, stripped of purpose. Most lose their sanity, transforming into shadows. At the deepest level lies **Oblivion**, an entity whose nature is to further dim the light of souls that reach it. Only those who retain their sanity must inevitably face Oblivion.
  - Not all Mu inhabitants are purposeless: some are friendly, others have compromised with the Mu. Oblivion counters the duo by sending powerful dark manifestations.

- **Gameplay Context**:
  - Enemies spawn, traps appear, and the map changes to hinder progress.
  - NPCs offer upgrades, trade, or clues.
  - Common enemies, setting, and lore items are collectible.

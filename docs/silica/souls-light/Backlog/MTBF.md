# MTBF

Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF) is a reliability metric quantifying the average time between system failures during continuous operation.

- Target MTBF for Souls Light: > 10 hours of uninterrupted gameplay.
- Crash rate target: < 0.01% of sessions due to unhandled exceptions.
- Validation method: stress-testing with automated playthroughs to measure cumulative uptime and failure frequency.
